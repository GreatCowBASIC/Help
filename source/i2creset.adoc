==== I2CReset

(((I2C Software,I2CReset)))


*Syntax:*
[source,gcbasic]
----
    I2CReset
----
*Command Availability:*

Available on all microcontrollers except 12 bit instruction Microchip PIC microcontrollers (10F,
12F5xx, 16F5xx chips)

*Explanation:*

This will attempt a reset of the I2C by changing the state of the I2C
bus.

*Example:*
[source,gcbasic]
----
    'Call if the I2C bus appears to be stuck, for example a slave device holding SDA low
    I2CReset          ' <<< the I2CReset instruction
----
*Key line:* `I2CReset` -- toggles the I2C bus lines in an attempt to clear a stuck or hung bus condition, such as a slave device holding SDA low.

Supported in <I2C.H>

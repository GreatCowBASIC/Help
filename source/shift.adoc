==== Shift

(((Variables Operations,Shift)))

*Syntax:*
[subs="specialcharacters,quotes"]
----
    Shift _variable_ [,] { Left | Right }
----

*Command Availability:*

Available on PIC and AVR microcontrollers.

*Explanation:*

The `Shift` command performs a logical shift of `_variable_` by one bit in the direction given. `_variable_` can be a Byte, Word or Long and is changed in place.

* A `0` is always shifted into the vacated bit: the least significant bit on `Left`, the most significant bit on `Right`.
* The bit shifted out is discarded. Unlike <<_rotate,Rotate>>, `Shift` does not feed the carry bit (`STATUS.C`) back into the variable, and the carry flag should not be relied on after the command.
* A `Left` shift multiplies an unsigned value by 2, and a `Right` shift divides it by 2.
* For a Word or Long the shift runs through every byte, so the bit shifted out of one byte is shifted into the next.
* `Left` and `Right` can be written `L` and `R`. The comma, and brackets round the variable, are optional: `Shift Value Left`, `Shift Value, Left` and `Shift (Value) Left` are the same.

`Shift` moves one bit each time it is used. To shift by several places, repeat it:

[source,gcbasic]
----
    Repeat 4
        Shift Value, Left
    End Repeat
----

`Shift` is faster than <<_fnlsl,FnLSL>> and <<_fnlsr,FnLSR>> for a shift of one place or a few places, because it is compiled in line and does not call a function. Use `FnLSL` or `FnLSR` when the number of places is held in a variable and the shifted value is needed inside an expression.

*Example:*

[source,gcbasic]
----
    ' Shift a byte and a word, left then right.
    ' Look at the values in the simulator, or send them to a terminal.

    #chip 16F18855, 32

    Dim TestByte As Byte
    Dim TestWord As Word

    TestByte = 0b10010010
    Shift TestByte, Left        ' <<< the Shift instruction: 0b00100100, the top bit is lost
    Shift TestByte, Right       ' 0b00010010, a 0 enters at the top

    TestWord = 0x0080
    Shift TestWord, Left        ' 0x0100: the bit moves from the low byte to the high byte
    Shift TestWord, Right       ' 0x0080

    End
----
*Key line:* `Shift TestWord, Left` -- moves every bit of the Word one place left through both bytes, a `0` entering at the bottom, with no carry fed back.

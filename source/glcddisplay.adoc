==== GLCDDisplay

(((Graphical LCD,GLCDDisplay)))


*Syntax:*
[source,gcbasic]
----

    GLCDDisplay Off | On

----
*Explanation:*

Places the GLCD in sleep mode or enables the GLCD for normal operations.


The options are:
[source,gcbasic]
----
    OFF
    ON
----
